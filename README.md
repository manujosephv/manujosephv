## 👨🏻‍💻 About Me
 
Manu Joseph is a self-made data scientist with 15+ years of cross-functional experience spanning analytics, machine learning, and AI research. He is a **Principal Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech India**, where he applies cutting-edge ML systems at the scale of one of the world's largest retailers. Previously, he headed Applied Research at **Thoucentric**, bridging AI research and customer applications.
 
He has extensive experience working with Fortune 500 companies on digital and AI transformations — particularly in **ML-based Demand Forecasting** — and has distilled that expertise into a [best-selling book on modern time series forecasting](https://www.amazon.com/Modern-Time-Forecasting-Python-industry-ready/dp/1803246804).
 
He is the creator and maintainer of **[PyTorch Tabular](https://github.com/manujosephv/pytorch_tabular)** (2,000+ ⭐, thousands of monthly downloads) — a framework that makes deep learning for tabular data simple and accessible. He is also a contributor to **NLTK**, where he enhanced the Language Model module with multiple statistical models.
 
His primary research interests are **time series**, **deep learning for tabular data**, and **LLMs**. He has published **6 papers on ArXiv** (h-index: 4, 98+ citations) and filed patents at Walmart in causal ML and conformal prediction.
 
Beyond the data, he is **Manu J** — author of *The Artist* (2024), a critically acclaimed psychological thriller praised by Dr. Shashi Tharoor — and the former creator of *Little Pajama Tales*, a children's bedtime podcast that reached families in 20+ countries.
 
📝 He writes about ML at **[deep-and-shallow.com](https://deep-and-shallow.com)** | 🏆 **AIM 40 Under 40 Data Scientists (2024)**
